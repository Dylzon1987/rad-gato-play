Privacy Policy

Rad Gato · updated 3 August 2026
Rad Gato does not collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no trackers. The game never sends information about you anywhere, because it never gathers any in the first place.

What is stored, and where

Rad Gato saves your progress — which level you reached, your star ratings, passport stamps, sound settings, and which words you have practiced — on your own device only. This information never leaves your phone. It is not backed up to us, shared, or sold, and we have no way to see it. Deleting the app deletes all of it permanently.

Network use

The game runs entirely offline. All artwork, audio and lessons are included in the app itself. Rad Gato makes no network requests during play.

Children

Rad Gato is family-friendly and safe for children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. There is no chat, no user-generated content, no social features, and no links out of the game.

Purchases

If a paid unlock is offered in a future version, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
